# Northstar Outfitters Product Guide

> Fictional demo content for Knowbase AI. Northstar Outfitters is not a real company.

## Summit 2P tent

The Summit 2P is a two-person, three-season backpacking tent. It weighs 1.6 kg packed
and pitches with a single hub pole. The rainfly has a 3000 mm waterproof rating.

## Ridgeline 45 backpack

The Ridgeline 45 is a 45-litre trekking backpack with an adjustable torso length and
a removable lid pocket. It weighs 1.4 kg and comes in two sizes, S/M and L/XL.

## Trailblazer 14 laptop

The Trailblazer 14 is a rugged 14-inch travel laptop. It weighs 1.3 kg, has a
battery rated for 12 hours of mixed use, and its keyboard resists light splashes. It
is not waterproof and must not be submerged.

## Ember stove

The Ember stove is a compact canister stove that boils one litre of water in about
four minutes. Fuel canisters are sold separately and are never shipped by express
delivery.
