# Northstar Outfitters Support FAQ

> Fictional demo content for Knowbase AI. Northstar Outfitters is not a real company.

## How do I contact support?

Use the support portal or email support@northstar.example. The support team answers
within one business day, Monday to Friday.

## Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders can be changed or cancelled within 2 hours of being placed. After that, the
order is already being packed; return it instead once it arrives.

## Do gift cards expire?

No. Northstar gift cards never expire and can be used on any product in the store.
Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Do you price match?

Northstar matches the price of an identical item from another authorised retailer
within 7 days of purchase. Price matching does not apply to clearance items.

## Where is my order?

Every shipped order has a tracking link in the shipping confirmation email. If the
tracking has not updated for 5 business days, contact support.
