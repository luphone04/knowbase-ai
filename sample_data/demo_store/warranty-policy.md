# Northstar Outfitters Warranty Policy

> Fictional demo content for Knowbase AI. Northstar Outfitters is not a real company.

## Standard warranty

Every Northstar tent carries a two-year warranty against manufacturing defects,
starting on the delivery date. Backpacks carry a five-year warranty. The Trailblazer
14 laptop carries a one-year limited warranty from the manufacturer.

## What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, accidents, normal wear, unauthorised
repairs, or water damage to electronics.

## NorthCare Plus

NorthCare Plus is an optional extended protection plan for the Trailblazer 14. It
extends coverage to three years in total and adds one accidental damage repair. It
must be purchased within 30 days of the laptop's delivery.

## Making a warranty claim

Open a warranty claim in the support portal with your order number and photos of the
defect. Approved claims are repaired or replaced within 10 business days. If a
product cannot be repaired, Northstar replaces it with the same model or one of equal
value.
