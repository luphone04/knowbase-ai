# Northstar Outfitters Returns Policy

> Fictional demo content for Knowbase AI. Northstar Outfitters is not a real company.

## Standard returns

Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the
original payment method. Items must be in their original packaging with all tags,
manuals, and accessories included.

## Laptops and electronics

Laptops, including the Trailblazer 14 travel laptop, can be returned within 14 days
of delivery if the factory seal is intact. Opened laptops can be returned within 14
days for a refund minus a 10% restocking fee. After 14 days, laptops are covered by
the warranty policy instead of the returns policy.

## How to start a return

Request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number from the support portal
before sending anything back. Write the RMA number on the outside of the box.
Returns without an RMA number cannot be processed and are sent back to the customer.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the warehouse inspects the item.
Original shipping charges are not refunded, except when the item arrived damaged or
we sent the wrong item.

## Items that cannot be returned

Gift cards, opened fuel canisters, and personalised items cannot be returned.
