# Northstar Outfitters Shipping Policy

> Fictional demo content for Knowbase AI. Northstar Outfitters is not a real company.

## Delivery options

Standard shipping takes three to five business days within the country. Express
shipping arrives the next business day when the order is placed before 12:00 noon
local time.

## Shipping costs

Standard shipping is free on orders over $50. Below $50, standard shipping costs
$6.95. Express shipping costs $19.95 for any order size.

## Order tracking

A tracking link is emailed as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse. Orders can
also be tracked in the support portal using the order number.

## International shipping

Northstar ships only within the country. International orders are not accepted.

## Damaged parcels

If a parcel arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours with photos. Northstar sends a
replacement at no cost and refunds the original shipping charge.
